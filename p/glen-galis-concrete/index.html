Good to know

Questions homeowners always ask

How much will my project cost?

Every home is different, so we give you a fixed, written quote after a free walkthrough. As a rough guide, most driveway replacements run $6k–$15k and patios $4k–$12k. We'll give you a real number, not a guess.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We partner with several home-improvement lenders and can walk you through monthly-payment options during your estimate, so a bigger project doesn't have to happen all at once.

Do you pull permits and handle inspections?

Always. Every job that needs a permit gets one, pulled in your municipality, with inspections scheduled and passed. It protects you at resale and keeps the work up to code.

How long will it take?

Most pours are done in 1–4 days; concrete then cures for about a week before it takes a car. You'll get a written timeline with your quote, and a project lead who tells you the moment anything changes.

What changes the price most?

Three things: moving walls or plumbing, the finish level you pick, and hidden damage we can't see until demo. The first two are priced in your written quote before we start. If we open a wall and find the third, work stops, you see photos and a price, and nothing proceeds until you approve it.

How long before we can use the new concrete?

You can walk on it in 1–2 days. Keep vehicles off a new driveway for at least 7 days so it cures to full strength. We leave clear cure instructions on every job.
