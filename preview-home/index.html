Questions

The ones I actually get.

Why is this so much cheaper than everyone else?

Two reasons, and neither is a catch. I am one person, not an agency with account managers and an office. And I have built this same kind of site 192 times, so the parts I would otherwise rebuild from scratch already exist. You are paying for the build, not for somebody's overhead.

Do you have clients I can call?

Not yet, and I am not going to pretend otherwise. Mason Web Works is new. What I can give you instead is every site I have built, live, above, so you can judge the work directly rather than take a reference's word for it. If that is not enough, that is a fair call to make.

What happens if I want changes next year?

Small edits, send them over and I do them. If you want a bigger rework later, we price that then. There is no plan you have to be on for me to pick up the phone.

Do I have to move my domain or my email?

No. The site can point at a domain you already own and your email keeps working exactly as it does now. If you would rather I handle the domain too, I can.

What do you need from me to start?

Photos of your actual work, your service area, your phone number, and twenty minutes to talk. If the photos are on your phone, that is fine, phone photos are what these sites are built around.

Who actually does the work?

I do. Vlad Protsenko, out of Elgin, Illinois. The person you talk to is the person building it, and nothing gets handed to a stranger.

Want to see yours before you decide?
