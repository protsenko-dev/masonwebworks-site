Where I actually fit

I build one kind of website: local service and home trades. Roofing, HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, remodeling, concrete, painting, tree work, flooring, fencing, masonry, exterior cleaning.

If your client list has contractors in it, you already know this is the slow, low margin end of your book. The client has twelve phone photos and no brand guide, the copy has to be written from a twenty minute call, and it still has to convert phone traffic. It is a bad fit for a team that does brand and campaign work, and a good fit for someone who has built the same thing 194 times.

How it runs

  1. You send a brief: business name, trade, service area, whatever photos and brand direction the client signed off on.
  2. I build and send you a live staging link, normally inside five business days.
  3. You review it as your own work and send revisions until it is right.
  4. You ship it under your name. I stay invisible.

In every build

  • Mobile first. Trades traffic is phone traffic, so the phone layout is the real design.
  • Click to call in the header, and a sticky call bar on mobile.
  • Two step quote form that gives the visitor an instant ballpark before it asks for contact details.
  • Google rating and reviews placed above the fold, where they change the call decision.
  • Local SEO scaffolding. Service area sections, LocalBusiness schema, clean meta.
  • Static HTML. No page builder bloat, so it loads fast and there is nothing to keep patched.
